Projecteds

Projected Items

Projected items are limiteds that have been intentionally sold at high prices to artificially inflate their RAP (Recent Average Price). This can make an item look more valuable than it really is to traders who don't understand projections.

Projections typically don't last long, since projected items usually sell far below their inflated RAP soon after. It's usually best to avoid trading for these items unless you're experienced enough to handle them safely.

About Heximons

What is Heximons?

Heximons is a fan-made Roblox limited item values and trading resource. It tracks RAP, value, demand and trend for limiteds so traders can make decisions with data instead of guesswork.

Who's behind it?

Heximons is built and maintained by a small hobby project team of traders who wanted a cleaner way to browse values, run trade calculations, and check trade ads in one place.

Get involved

Values are only useful when the community helps keep them honest. Join the Discord to report bad values, suggest features, or just talk trading.
